About Hetavi Patel

Founded with a passion for the environment, Hetavi Patel focuses on innovative, sustainable solutions that reduce waste, conserve resources, and inspire communities to live greener lives.

We believe that small changes can make a big impact, and we strive to lead by example through our eco-conscious products and services.

Our Services
Eco Product Design

Creating sustainable, biodegradable, and reusable products that minimize environmental impact.

Waste Reduction Consulting

Helping businesses and communities implement effective waste reduction and recycling programs.

Renewable Energy Solutions

Advising on and installing solar and other renewable energy systems to reduce carbon footprints.

Integrity:   Transparency and honesty guide our work and relationships.
